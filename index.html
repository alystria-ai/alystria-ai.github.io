03 · How they're made

Small studio, big feelings.

  1. i.

    Free, and open to read

    Things are released for free, and the code is open so anyone can learn from it, fork it or build their own characters on top.

  2. ii.

    On your machine, if you like

    Where it is possible, characters think and speak locally on your own PC. No account, no key, no internet connection.

  3. iii.

    Presence over polish

    Lip sync, eye contact, a remark about the fight you just won. The small details that make someone feel like they are actually there.

  4. iv.

    Made by one person

    Alystria is a personal studio. Fewer things, made carefully, and shared as they are built.
